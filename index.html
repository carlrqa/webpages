Choose your path — an interactive video pitch

The intro plays inline and muted. When it ends, the viewer picks a branch ("I'm in Sales" / "I'm in IT"), gets a tailored clip, and can hit a CTA or unlock the full demo behind a quick form. Every choice fires an analytics event.

Analytics events will appear here as you interact…

What's happening under the hood

Each clip is a node. choices branch to other nodes by id; a node with a form gates the next clip behind lead capture; and an onEvent callback receives play, ended, choice, cta, and lead events.
